This is a static, browser-local planning dashboard for YouTube Funnels. It does not connect to Meta, spend money, publish ads or update any external account.

No verified company website or Meta export was supplied. The brand mark, reserved `.example` destination, campaign hierarchy, IDs and performance metrics are original synthetic planning material, not live or historical advertising data.

The six 1254 × 1254 creative images were generated specifically for this dashboard around content-engine agency concepts. They are not exported Meta assets.
